### Obsidian

Before AI even took over, I preferred taking notes in Markdown, and Obsidian is
the best app for doing so. There is also no vendor lock-in, and the creator
[@kepano](https://x.com/kepano) strongly advocates for [file over
app](https://stephango.com/file-over-app).

### Brave Browser

I started using Brave several years ago as I have always been an advocate for
digital privacy, and I believe Google is one of the worst violators of that.
Brave works just as well as Chrome while offering me improved privacy and
security. When Brave Search was released, I became fully locked. I will admit,
Google's search is better at times, but Brave Search continues to improve and
with the advent of new AI capabilities, my traditional search volume is
decreasing.
